Supply Chain Lifecycle Treaceability Index

Supply chains generate enormous volumes of operational data, yet most organisations still struggle to reconstruct what actually happened to a product, material, asset, or shipment across its full lifecycle. The structural failure is remarkably consistent across industries:

This index is representative, not exhaustive. It demonstrates how lifecycle traceability creates different forms of operational, financial, strategic, and trust-based value across different supply chain archetypes. In some industries the issue is provenance. In others it is emissions, authenticity, recall precision, lifecycle time, asset utilisation, regulatory defensibility, resilience, or circularity. What changes between industries is the economic expression of the problem. What remains consistent is the need to convert fragmented operational events into continuous, verifiable lifecycle intelligence. Our solution, the Value Chain Integrator (VCI), addresses this gap by creating an event-level chain of custody across organisations, products, assets, locations, and time. The opportunity is not simply better visibility. It is the ability to coordinate supply chains around what actually happened, what is happening now, and what can be trusted for decision-making. Traceability is not a niche compliance capability. It is a cross-industry operational and financial coordination layer.
