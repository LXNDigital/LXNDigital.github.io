# Operational Continuity Diagnostic Workbench Users Guide

---

# Introduction

## About the Workbench

The Operational Continuity Diagnostic Workbench is a structured assessment application designed to help organisations identify operational continuity signals that may be influencing performance.

The application supports two assessment stages:

1. Executive Pre-Assessment
2. Full Diagnostic

Assessment results are analysed to identify patterns, continuity themes, and potential areas for further investigation.

The workbench is designed as a decision-support tool and should be used alongside organisational judgement, stakeholder input, and operational evidence.

Resetting an assessment will remove previously recorded responses for that assessment.

# Executive Pre-Assessment

## Purpose

The Executive Pre-Assessment provides a rapid method for identifying potential continuity signals across the organisation.

It is intended to help determine whether a deeper assessment through the Full Diagnostic may be warranted.

## Response Scale

Each question uses a five-point response scale.

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| Strongly Disagree | Condition not present |
| Disagree | Condition occasionally occurs but is not significant |
| Neutral | Insufficient information or uncertainty |
| Agree | Condition appears present |
| Strongly Agree | Condition appears significant or persistent |

## Triggered Indicators

Indicators receiving:

- Agree
- Strongly Agree

are considered triggered indicators.

Triggered indicators are used to:

- Highlight areas of interest
- Support report generation

## Completing the Assessment

1. Open Executive Pre-Assessment.
2. Review each indicator.
3. Select the response that best reflects current conditions.
4. Save progress as required.
5. Submit the assessment.
6. Review generated results.

# Full Diagnostic

## Purpose

The Full Diagnostic explores selected continuity signals in greater detail.

It is designed to provide additional context and improve understanding of assessment findings.

## How Scope Is Determined

The Full Diagnostic does not necessarily present every available indicator.

Question availability depends on:

- Executive Pre-Assessment findings
- Triggered indicators
- Licence entitlements

As a result, different organisations may see different question sets.

## Completing the Full Diagnostic

1. Open Full Diagnostic.
2. Review available indicators.
3. Complete all applicable questions.
4. Submit the assessment.
5. Generate the Full Diagnostic report.

# Understanding Diagnostic Lenses

The workbench evaluates indicators across five diagnostic lenses.

## Financial Absorption

Examines situations where uncertainty may be absorbed through financial mechanisms.

Examples:

- Inventory buffers
- Working capital
- Premium freight
- Contingency reserves

---

## Reconstruction

Examines effort required to reconstruct operational information.

Examples:

- Reconciliation
- Information recovery
- Investigation effort
- Manual consolidation

---

## Resilience Economics

Examines the cost and complexity of maintaining resilience.

Examples:

- Redundancy
- Duplicate processes
- Operational buffering
- Resilience expenditure

---

## Decision Latency

Examines situations where uncertainty may delay action.

Examples:

- Delayed approvals
- Escalations
- Extended decision cycles
- Additional verification

---

## Defensibility

Examines confidence in evidence, traceability, and organisational claims.

Examples:

- Evidence confidence
- Audit preparation effort
- Traceability limitations
- Assurance complexity

# Understanding Results

## Executive Summary

Provides a high-level overview of assessment outcomes.

This section highlights:

- Significant observations
- Continuity themes
- Areas for investigation

---

## Priority Findings

Priority Findings identify the observations that appear most significant based on assessment responses.

Each finding includes:

- Lens
- Indicator
- Observation
- Interpretation

---

## Cross-Functional Observations

Identifies themes that appear across:

- Multiple indicators
- Multiple lenses
- Multiple organisational functions

These observations may provide additional context regarding broader continuity conditions.

---

## Structural Interpretation

Provides a higher-level interpretation of assessment patterns.

Structural interpretations should be viewed as hypotheses rather than conclusions.

---

## Recommended Pilot Pathway

The Recommended Pilot Pathway identifies a practical area for further investigation.

Pilot pathways are intended to help organisations move from observation to evidence.

A pilot pathway is not a recommendation to purchase a solution. It identifies a practical area where additional investigation may help validate observations, improve understanding, or generate evidence regarding continuity conditions.

Examples may include:

- Inventory and working capital continuity
- Supplier continuity
- Decision confidence
- Operational assurance
- Lifecycle visibility

---

## Detailed Findings

Detailed Findings provide indicator-level analysis including:

- Indicator identifier
- Assessment response
- Interpretation
- Potential implications

# Privacy and Data Storage

## Local-First Operation

The workbench is designed to operate locally on the user's device.

Assessment processing, report generation, and analysis occur locally.

## Assessment Data

Assessment responses are stored locally.

The application does not automatically transmit assessment responses to LXNDigital.

## Reports

Generated PDF reports are stored locally.

Users remain responsible for report management, retention, and distribution.

## Licensing

Licence activation uses device-specific information only.

Assessment responses are not included in licence requests.

## Data Storage Locations

The application stores all information locally.

Examples include:

- Assessment responses
- Licence information
- Application configuration information
- User preferences

Storage locations vary by operating system.

Users should ensure local data is included in organisational backup procedures where appropriate.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is the workbench an audit?

No.

The workbench is a diagnostic and decision-support application.

---

## Is the workbench a compliance assessment?

No.

The workbench does not assess compliance against regulations or standards.

---

## Is the workbench a maturity assessment?

No.

The workbench focuses on identifying continuity signals rather than assigning maturity levels.

---

## What does Neutral mean?

Neutral generally indicates:

- Insufficient information
- Limited visibility
- Uncertainty regarding current conditions

---

## Does a triggered indicator mean something is wrong?

Not necessarily.

Triggered indicators identify conditions that may warrant further investigation.

---

## Why don't I receive a maturity score?

The workbench focuses on interpretation and continuity signals rather than organisational grading.

---

## Why are some Full Diagnostic questions unavailable?

Question availability is determined by:

- Triggered indicators
- Assessment scope
- Licence entitlements

---

## Does the application require internet access?

No.

Core assessment functionality operates locally.

Internet access may be required only for support or licence-related activities.

---

## Does LXNDigital receive my assessment responses?

No.

Assessment information remains on the local device unless explicitly shared by the organisation.

# Disclaimer

The Operational Continuity Diagnostic Workbench is intended to support discussion, investigation, and organisational understanding.

The application identifies signals and patterns based on assessment responses.

It does not:

- Determine root causes
- Assign responsibility
- Provide assurance
- Certify compliance
- Prescribe solutions

Assessment outputs should always be interpreted alongside organisational context, operational evidence, and professional judgement.
